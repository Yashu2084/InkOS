Operating Layer for Human Intent

"The most natural interface is the one you don't notice. InkOS turns the simple act of drawing into a direct conversation with computational context."

InkOS was founded on a simple realization: modern computer interaction is heavily fragmented. When you see an equation, an unknown French phrase, or a product in a photograph on your screen, you are forced to copy-paste, open search browsers, type prompts, and flip windows.

By overlaying a universal, high-performance canvas directly above all running software, InkOS interprets coordinate intersections and matches drawing gestures in less than 10 milliseconds. Powered by a Rust core runtime, local on-device models, and WebAssembly plugin nodes, InkOS is built for speed and complete operational privacy.
